# Reusable Protocols 

Below you will find downloadable versions (most available in both .docx and .pdf) of RRCHNM’s sustainability protocols and documents, which can be reused as models in other contexts:

- Sustainability Guide ( {{< doc-link href="/files/Sustainability-Guide.docx" text="docx" name="Sustainability Guide" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/Sustainability-Guide.pdf" text="pdf" name="Sustainability Guide" >}} )
  - high-level overview of what RRCHNM sustains and how it re-evaluates its sustained projects on a regular basis
- Server Sustainability Policy ({{< doc-link href="/files/Server-Sustainability-Policy.docx" text="docx" name="Server Sustainability Policy" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/Server-Sustainability-Policy.pdf" text="pdf" name="Server Sustainability Policy" >}})
  - the kinds of projects RRCHNM sustains on its servers and for how long
- Minimum Hosting Requirements ({{< doc-link href="/files/Minimum-Hosting-Requirements.docx" text="docx" name="Minimum Hosting Requirements" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/End-of-project-workflow.pdf" text="pdf" name="Minimum Hosting Requirements" >}})
  - requirements for projects to be safely hosted on RRCHNM servers
- General End of Project Workflow ({{< doc-link href="/files/End-of-project-workflow.docx" text="docx" name="General End of Project Workflow" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/End-of-project-workflow.pdf" text="pdf" name="General End of Project Workflow" >}})
  - workflow guide to transition projects into sustainability mode at the end of active funding or when triaging recently ended projects
- MARS Upload instructions ({{< doc-link href="/files/MARS-Upload-Instructions.docx" text="docx" name="MARS Upload instructions" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/MARS-Upload-Instructions.pdf" text="pdf" name="MARS Upload instructions" >}})
  - how RRCHNM records information about projects and archives copies of project information in our institutional repository
- MARS Upload instructions for podcasts ({{< doc-link href="/files/PodcastMARSUploadInstructions.docx" text="docx" name="MARS Upload instructions for podcasts" >}} / {{< doc-link href="/files/PodcastMARSUploadInstructions.pdf" text="pdf" name="MARS Upload instructions for podcasts" >}})
  - how RRCHNM and R2 Studios records information and archives copies of podcast episodes and shows
- Sample Tracking Sheet ({{< doc-link href="/files/Sample-Tracking-Sheet.xlsx" text="xlsx" name="Sample Tracking Sheet" >}})
  - example of tracking sheet used when sustaining and doing an annual triage on multiple (dozens, hundreds) of projects
